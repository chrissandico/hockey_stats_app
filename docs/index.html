Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 21, 2026

Your Privacy Matters: The Shift is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

1. Introduction

Welcome to The Shift ("we," "our," or "us"). The Shift is an offline-first hockey statistics tracking application that helps teams track game statistics and sync them to Google Sheets. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

By using The Shift, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We collect the following personal information when you use The Shift:

  • Email Address: Collected through Google Sign-In for authentication and account management
  • Google Account Information: Basic profile information from your Google account (name, profile picture) used for authentication
  • Device Identifiers: Device ID and session tokens for authentication and security purposes

2.2 Game and Statistics Data

The Shift collects and stores the following game-related data that you enter:

  • Game Information: Game dates, opponents, locations, game types
  • Player Information: Player names, jersey numbers, positions, team assignments
  • Game Statistics: Shots, goals, assists, penalties, attendance records, line configurations
  • Goalie Statistics: Saves, goals against, save percentages
  • Team Information: Team names, logos, colors, configurations

2.3 Technical Information

  • App Usage Data: Features used, sync status, error logs (stored locally)
  • Device Information: Operating system version, app version, device type (for troubleshooting)

2.4 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect your location data
  • We do NOT collect payment information (the app is free)
  • We do NOT track your browsing history
  • We do NOT collect data from other apps on your device
  • We do NOT use cookies or tracking pixels

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Primary Uses

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • App Functionality: To provide core stat tracking features and offline storage
  • Authentication: To verify your identity and manage your account access
  • Data Synchronization: To sync your statistics to your Google Sheets
  • Multi-Team Management: To maintain separate data for different teams you manage
  • Data Storage: To store your data locally on your device and in your Google Sheets

3.2 Secondary Uses

  • Technical Support: To diagnose and fix technical issues you report
  • App Improvement: To understand how features are used and improve the app
  • Security: To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or security issues

3.3 What We Do NOT Do With Your Data

  • We do NOT sell your data to third parties
  • We do NOT use your data for advertising
  • We do NOT share your data with marketing companies
  • We do NOT use your data to train AI models
  • We do NOT send promotional emails (unless you opt-in)

4. How We Store and Protect Your Information

4.1 Local Storage

Your game statistics are stored locally on your device using encrypted Hive database. This allows the app to work offline. Local data is protected by your device's security features.

4.2 Cloud Storage

When you sync, your data is stored in YOUR Google Sheets account. We do not store your statistics on our servers. You maintain full control over your Google Sheets data, including:

  • Who can access your sheets
  • How long data is retained
  • When to delete data
  • Export and backup options

4.3 Data Transmission Security

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between the app and Google Sheets is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Secure Authentication: We use Google OAuth 2.0 for secure authentication
  • No Intermediate Storage: Data goes directly from your device to your Google Sheets

4.4 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission
  • Secure credential storage using Flutter Secure Storage
  • Team-specific authentication and data isolation
  • Regular security updates

Important: While we implement strong security measures, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

5.1 Google Sheets

Your statistics data is synced to YOUR Google Sheets account. This is the core functionality of the app. You control who can access your Google Sheets through Google's sharing settings.

5.2 Team Members

If you share your Google Sheets with team members, coaches, or parents, they will have access to the statistics data according to the permissions you grant in Google Sheets.

5.3 Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In: For authentication (Google's Privacy Policy applies)
  • Google Sheets API: For data synchronization (Google's Privacy Policy applies)
  • Google Play Services: For app distribution and updates (Google's Privacy Policy applies)

5.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., court orders, subpoenas).

5.5 Business Transfers

If The Shift is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred. We will provide notice before your information is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Access Your Data

You can access all your statistics data at any time through:

  • The app's statistics view screens
  • Your Google Sheets (where all data is synced)
  • Exporting data from Google Sheets

6.2 Modify Your Data

You can modify your statistics data through:

  • The app's edit features
  • Directly editing your Google Sheets

6.3 Delete Your Data

You have the right to delete your data:

  • Local Data: Uninstall the app or use the app's reset feature
  • Google Sheets Data: Delete rows or entire sheets in your Google Sheets
  • Account Data: Revoke app access in your Google Account settings
  • Complete Deletion: Contact us at chris@professormeta.io for assistance

6.4 Export Your Data

You can export your data at any time:

  • Download your Google Sheets as Excel, CSV, or PDF
  • Use the app's PDF report generation feature
  • Use the app's email export feature

6.5 Revoke Access

You can revoke The Shift's access to your Google account at any time:

  1. Go to your Google Account settings
  2. Navigate to "Security" → "Third-party apps with account access"
  3. Find "The Shift" and click "Remove Access"

6.6 Opt-Out of Communications

We do not send marketing emails. Any emails you receive from us are transactional (e.g., password resets, support responses). You cannot opt-out of these essential communications.

7. Children's Privacy

The Shift is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is designed for:

  • Adult team administrators
  • Coaches (18+)
  • Stat trackers (18+)
  • Parents viewing statistics

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at chris@professormeta.io, and we will delete such information.

Note: While the app may track statistics for youth hockey players (including those under 13), the app itself should be operated by adults (coaches, parents, or adult stat trackers).

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and maintained on servers located outside of your state, province, country, or other governmental jurisdiction where data protection laws may differ.

If you are located outside the United States and choose to use The Shift, your data will be processed in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable laws.

9. Data Retention

9.1 How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Local Data: Stored on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data
  • Google Sheets Data: Stored indefinitely in your Google Sheets until you delete it
  • Authentication Tokens: Stored until you sign out or revoke access

9.2 Automatic Deletion

We do not automatically delete your data. You maintain full control over data retention through your Google Sheets.
