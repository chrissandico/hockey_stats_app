## Full Description (4000 characters max)

```
The Shift is the ultimate offline-first hockey statistics tracker designed for live game tracking. Record every shot, goal, penalty, and player action without needing internet—perfect for arenas with poor connectivity. Your stats automatically sync to Google Sheets when you're back online, giving coaches and parents instant access through a web-based portal.

🏒 PERFECT FOR LIVE GAMES
Track stats in real-time during games without worrying about internet connection. The Shift works completely offline, storing everything locally until you're ready to sync. Never miss recording a crucial play because of connectivity issues.

⚡ KEY FEATURES

OFFLINE-FIRST TRACKING
• Record shots, goals, assists, and penalties without internet
• All data stored locally on your device
• Automatic background sync when online
• Never lose data—even if connection drops mid-game

COMPREHENSIVE STAT TRACKING
• Goals with assist tracking (primary and secondary)
• Shot tracking with on-goal indicators
• Penalty logging with type and duration
• Player attendance management
• Line configuration and tracking
• Goalie statistics (saves, goals against, save percentage)
• Period-by-period tracking (P1, P2, P3, OT)
• Goal situations (Even Strength, Power Play, Short Handed)

GOOGLE SHEETS INTEGRATION
• Automatic sync to your team's Google Sheet
• Real-time collaboration—multiple trackers can work simultaneously
• Standardized data format for easy analysis
• Access your data anywhere through Google Sheets
• Export and share with coaching staff

MULTI-TEAM SUPPORT
• Manage multiple teams with separate data
• Team-specific authentication and security
• Switch between teams seamlessly
• Perfect for coaches managing multiple squads

COACHES & PARENTS PORTAL
• Web-based access to view all game statistics
• Real-time updates as stats are tracked
• Review historical game data
• Share access with parents and coaching staff
• No app installation required for viewing

PROFESSIONAL FEATURES
• PDF report generation for game summaries
• Email reports directly from the app
• Native sharing to any platform
• Player selection with search and filtering
• Edit and correct stats after logging
• View unsynced events before they upload
• Connectivity status indicator

🎯 WHO IT'S FOR

STAT TRACKERS & SCOREKEEPERS
Track every play with confidence, knowing your data is safe even without internet. Focus on the game, not connectivity.

COACHES
Get real-time insights during games. Review stats between periods. Make data-driven decisions about lines and strategies.

TEAM ADMINISTRATORS
Manage multiple teams efficiently. Control access and permissions. Maintain consistent stat tracking across all games.

PARENTS
View your player's stats in real-time through the web portal. No app installation needed—just open the link and watch the stats update live.

📊 HOW IT WORKS

1. SIGN IN: Authenticate with your Google account
2. SELECT GAME: Choose or create a game to track
3. TRACK STATS: Record shots, goals, penalties during the game (works offline)
4. AUTO-SYNC: Data syncs to Google Sheets when you're back online
5. SHARE ACCESS: Coaches and parents view stats through web portal

🔒 PRIVACY & SECURITY

• Your data stays in YOUR Google Sheets
• Team-specific authentication for data isolation
• Encrypted data transmission
• No ads, no data selling
• You control who has access

💡 WHY THE SHIFT?

Traditional stat tracking apps fail when arena WiFi is spotty or non-existent. The Shift was built specifically for this challenge—track everything offline, sync when you can. Plus, with Google Sheets integration, your data is accessible anywhere, by anyone you choose to share with.

Whether you're tracking stats rinkside, coaching from the bench, or reviewing game data at home, The Shift gives you the tools you need without the connectivity headaches.

🚀 GET STARTED TODAY

Download The Shift and transform how you track hockey statistics. Perfect for youth hockey, adult leagues, high school teams, and competitive clubs.

Track offline. Sync anywhere. Win more games.
```
